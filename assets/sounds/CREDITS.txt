INNERT SOUND CREDITS
====================

Every sound effect in Innert is made from the recordings below. All of them are released under
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): free to use, change and give away, for any
purpose, with no conditions. They're credited here anyway, with thanks to the people who made them.

The files in this folder were trimmed, layered, pitched, filtered and brought to an even loudness
from these recordings, then built into the game. None of them come from commercial games.

Freesound sounds were downloaded from each sound's page, where its licence is shown as CC0; Kenney's
packs say CC0 on their pages and in their own licence files.
